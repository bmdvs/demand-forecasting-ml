# Deviations

Realism checks that fall outside their expected range, with the measured value, the range, and how each is handled. A check listed here keeps running with its original range.

| Check | Range | Measured | Since | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjustment share of quantity moved (%) | 15.0 to 25.0 | 26.8 | before the demand signal update (archived reference, commit `91392ce`) | Pre-existing. `T2_ADJ_SHARE_OF_QTY` is not retuned. The check does not gate a run on its own, but a run that moves it more than 3 percentage points from 26.8 in either direction (below 23.8 or above 29.8) stops for review, because that would mean a change elsewhere is affecting the ledger. |
| Service lines backordered, forward window under the model (%) | at most 1.5 (amended from "under 1"; the 98.5% fill target for service-critical parts) | 1.9 (Run 03), 2.2 (Run 04) | Run 03 | Residual. Backordered lines by run: 21 (Run 02), 28 (Run 03), 33 (Run 04), of about 1,500, almost all on parts no demand-signal mechanism touches. 1.5% is about 22 lines and the run-to-run standard deviation is about 5 lines. Shared cause with the model variant's forward inventory: the current feature set has no calendar or hold awareness, so the plant calendar and service holds raise the model's bias corrections and lot sizes. Expected at or under 1.5% after the S3 and S6 rungs of the ladder; otherwise a finding for the report. The check stays at 1.5%. |
| Switches beginning with a successor stockout (%) | under 10 | 15.2 | Run 05 | Residual explained by the M2 drift set. 5 of 33 switches open with a successor shortage within 14 days. 3 of the 5 are parts in the planted lead-time drift set, whose stale master lead time delays the successor's first receipt past the switch (MEC-00220, HDW-00571, HDW-01105). Outside the drift set the share is 2 of 33 (6.1%): ELE-00014, where the old stock fell short on a single requirement and the first order went out the same day, and ELE-01158, whose first receipt took 61 days against a 54-day master lead. The check stays at 10%. |
